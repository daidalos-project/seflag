# English Version
**General Notes**

- The questions used in this template in general are short versions of the questions proposed in Gebru et al., meaning they are largely verbatim copies of at least those parts, which are set in bold face in Gebru et al.

- The thematic sections of Gebru et al. are (slightly) renamed and reordered in the template (leicht) and some of the questions have been located in different sections or appear in a different order than in the Gebru et al. paper

**Deviating ordering of questions compared to Gebru et al.**

- „Does the dataset contain data that might be considered confidential (e.g., data that is protected by legal privilege or by doctorpatient confidentiality, data that includes the content of individuals’ non-public communications)? If so, please provide a description.“ as well as the four following questions in Gebru et al. → In Gebru et al. placed in sections _Composition_, question 20–24 in the data sheet under _Dataset Creation and Content (legal, ethical)_

- „How was the data associated with each instance acquired?„ → Questions 17 in the template

- „What mechanisms or procedures were used to collect the data \[…]“ → Question 14 in the template

- „If the dataset is a sample from a larger set, what was the sampling strategy “ → Question 15 in th e template

- „Over what timeframe was the data collected?“ → Question 16 in the template

- „Were any ethical review processes conducted (e.g., by an institutional review board)?“ → Question 18 in the template

- „Who was involved in the data collection process […]?“ → Question 19 in the template

- „Did you collect the data from the individuals in question directly […]“ as well as the four following questions → Questions 26–29 in the template (Questions 26 combines two of the Gebru et al. questions).

- *Uses* Section of Gebru et al. has been moved to *Part III: Uses* in the templat. *Distribution* and *Maintenance* are placed before *Part III: Uses* in the template.

- Question 46 combines two Questions from Gebru et al. („Is there a repository that links to any or all papers or systems that use the dataset? “ + „What (other) tasks could the dataset be used for?“)
